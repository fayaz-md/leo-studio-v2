# Leo Studio V3 Master Plan

## Vision

Leo Studio is an AI-powered cartoon production platform that creates
high-quality educational and entertainment videos with persistent
characters, reusable worlds, and automated video generation.

---

# Core Principles

1. AI creates stories.
2. Leo Studio owns the universe.
3. Characters are permanent.
4. Voices are permanent.
5. Locations are permanent.
6. Props are permanent.
7. Every episode builds on previous episodes.
8. Everything is configurable.
