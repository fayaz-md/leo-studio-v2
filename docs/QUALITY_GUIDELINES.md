# 🎬 Leo Studio - Quality Guidelines

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

This document defines the production quality standards for every Leo Adventures episode.

Every new feature added to Leo Studio should improve one or more of these quality areas.

---

# Episode Goals

Every episode should:

- Capture attention within the first 3 seconds.
- Tell a complete story.
- Be emotionally engaging.
- Be visually dynamic.
- End with a memorable Leo message.

Target audience:

Children (4–10 years)

---

# Story Quality

## Opening Hook

The first 3 seconds are the most important.

Avoid:

> एक दिन...

Instead begin with:

- A question
- A surprise
- A danger
- A mystery
- A funny situation

Examples:

"अरे! ये खरगोश इतना डरा हुआ क्यों है?"

"रुको! जंगल में कुछ अजीब हो रहा है!"

---

## Story Structure

Every episode should follow:

Hook

↓

Problem

↓

Adventure

↓

Climax

↓

Happy Ending

↓

Leo Outro

---

## Story Length

Preferred:

45–60 seconds

Maximum:

60 seconds

Narration should be short.

Characters should speak more than the narrator.

---

# Character Quality

Leo must always:

- Be kind.
- Be brave.
- Help others.
- Stay cheerful.
- Encourage friendship.

Leo should never behave selfishly or cruelly.

---

## Character Conversations

Avoid long narration.

Prefer conversations.

Example:

Narrator

↓

Rabbit

↓

Leo

↓

Rabbit

↓

Leo

↓

Narrator

---

# Visual Quality

Every scene should include movement.

Examples:

- Slow Zoom In
- Slow Zoom Out
- Pan Left
- Pan Right

Static scenes should be avoided whenever possible.

---

## Scene Composition

Every image should:

- Keep Leo visually consistent.
- Have expressive facial emotions.
- Have cinematic lighting.
- Clearly show the story action.

---

## Thumbnail Moment

Every episode should contain at least one scene suitable for a YouTube thumbnail.

Examples:

- Leo surprised
- Leo smiling
- Animal crying
- Big discovery
- Treasure
- Giant animal

---

# Audio Quality

Narration should be energetic.

Characters should have individual voices.

Future improvements:

- Emotion-based voices
- Background music
- Sound effects
- Ambient sounds

---

# Video Quality

Transitions should be smooth.

Preferred transitions:

- Fade
- Cross Fade

Camera movement should be slow and cinematic.

---

# Ending

Every episode should end with Leo's signature sign-off:

"दोस्तों, आज की कहानी आपको कैसी लगी?

अगली बार लियो किसके साथ मस्ती करे? कमेंट में अपना जवाब ज़रूर लिखिए!

वीडियो पसंद आया हो तो Like करें और चैनल को Subscribe करना मत भूलिए!

फिर मिलेंगे लियो की अगली मज़ेदार एडवेंचर में। बाय!"

---

# Future Goals

Leo Studio should eventually produce videos that require little or no manual editing while maintaining:

- Character consistency
- Story consistency
- High visual quality
- High audio quality
- Professional YouTube Shorts pacing

---

# Leo Studio Philosophy

We are not generating random AI videos.

We are building a reusable AI animation studio capable of producing consistent, engaging and emotionally rich animated episodes.
